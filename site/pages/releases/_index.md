The [release board](@/releases/board.md) records what we intend to ship.
The [pipeline graphs](@/releases/pipelines.md) explain how configured publication happens.
The [platform matrix](@/releases/platforms.md) distinguishes policy from successful validation.
The [change log](@/releases/changes.md) records reviewed infrastructure migrations with their commits.

**Unknown is a useful state.** There is no automatic green checkmark for a Cargo version,
a workflow file, a repository's existence, or an absent blocker record.
