The [release board](@/releases/board.md) is what we intend to ship and what is in the way.
[Pipelines](@/releases/pipelines.md) show how a tag becomes artifacts. The
[platform matrix](@/releases/platforms.md) separates what is configured from what has
actually been proven, and the [change log](@/releases/changes.md) says what moved under
consumers recently.

Unknown is a legitimate state here. A Cargo version, a workflow file or the absence of a
blocker does not earn a green checkmark.
